# EXECUTIVE PITCH DECK STRUCTURE: TRUE CRIME FORMAT
## Слайд-дека для презентации руководству медиакомпании и спонсорам

> **Цель:** 10-минутная презентация проекта менеджменту для утверждения бюджета на пилотный эпизод.

---

### SLIDE 1: THE TITLE & CORE PROPOSITION
* **Title:** THE TURNING POINT (or: The Anatomy of Motive)
* **Tagline:** True Crime Re-engineered: Human Psychology Over Ghoulish Sensationalism.
* **Presented by:** Editorial & Production Lead

### SLIDE 2: THE MARKET VOID (THE "WHY NOW?")
* **The Problem:** True Crime on YouTube is oversaturated with repetitive serial killer recaps, amateur "Wikipedia readings", and cheap AI slop that viewers and advertisers increasingly reject.
* **The Opportunity:** A growing demand for **intelligent, presenter-led analytical investigations** (benchmarks: Sasha Sulim, JCS, Matt Orchard) focusing on relationship dynamics, hidden motives, and psychological red flags.
* **Target Audience:** 22–45 age demographic (55% female, 45% male), high CPM / ad-revenue tier (finance, mental health, cybersecurity, VPN, legal education sponsors).

### SLIDE 3: EDITORIAL PILLARS (HOW WE WIN)
1. **Primary Sources Only:** Built on PACER dockets, police bodycam, and 911 audio — zero uncited rumors.
2. **Behavioral Psychology:** Explaining *why* people snapped, not just *how* they killed.
3. **Rotating Expert Pool:** Certified forensic psychologists, former prosecutors, and detectives via pro-bono book partnerships.
4. **Cinematic Studio Aesthetic:** High production value delivered via an agile, remote pipeline.

### SLIDE 5: PILOT EPISODE OVERVIEW
* **Case:** *The Illusion of Control (The Whispering Pines Disappearance)*
* **Format Mechanics Demonstrated:** 
  - Cold Open hook in 90 seconds.
  - Interactive digital timeline of red flags.
  - Interrogation video breakdown.
  - 2 expert remote syncs (Forensic Psychologist + Digital Forensics).
  - Ethical takeaways & victim crisis helpline resources.

### SLIDE 6: BUDGET & RESOURCE ALLOCATION
* **Capital Setup (One-time):** Studio camera/lighting/mic package ($2,500 – $4,000) OR leased studio slot.
* **Per-Episode Variable Cost:** 
  - Primary Court Records / PACER / FOIA fees: $50–$150.
  - Remote Editor & Motion Designer: $800–$1,400.
  - Expert micro-honorariums (if applicable): $150.
  - Total per episode: ~$1,000 – $1,700 (vs. $20k+ for traditional TV doc).

### SLIDE 7: PATH TO MONETIZATION & ROI
* **Phase 1 (Months 1–3):** YouTube AdSense revenue optimization (high Watch Time on 30-minute videos).
* **Phase 2 (Months 4–6):** Direct brand integrations (BetterHelp, NordVPN, MasterClass, Audible, SimpliSafe).
* **Phase 3 (Month 6+):** Audio podcast spin-off (zero additional recording cost, purely exporting host audio + expert syncs).
